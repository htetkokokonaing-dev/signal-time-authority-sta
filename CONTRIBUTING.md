# Contributing and Feedback

Thank you for your interest in the Signal-Time-Authority (STA) Series.

At this stage, the main goal of this repository is to collect feedback, clarify the framework, and document future research directions.

## Useful Feedback

Helpful feedback includes:

- unclear definitions,
- possible overclaims,
- missing assumptions,
- technical weaknesses,
- simulation limitations,
- implementation risks,
- governance concerns,
- related work suggestions,
- wording or presentation improvements.

## Current Scope

The STA Series PublicRelease v1.0 is frozen as a first public release.

Please treat proposed changes as feedback for future versions, not as immediate edits to the archived Zenodo records.

## Non-Claim

STA is not presented as a proof of AI safety and is not a replacement for domain-specific safety standards, certification, regulator review, or professional governance processes.
